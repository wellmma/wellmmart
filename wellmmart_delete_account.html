Delete your Wellmmart account and data

Wellmmart — wellness & healthy products marketplace (Developer: Wellmma)

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app or website

  1. Open Wellmmart and sign in.
  2. Scroll to the bottom (footer) and open Account → Delete Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm. Your profile, saved details and order history are removed and you are signed out.

For security, if your sign-in session is old the app may ask you to sign in again before deleting.

Option 2 — Request by email

Email us from your registered email address with the subject “Delete my account” and we will remove your account and every associated record, then confirm once it is done.

Email developer.saadh@gmail.com

We verify ownership of the account before acting, then confirm by reply. Requests are processed within 7 days.

What data is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (name, email, phone number).
  • Your saved delivery addresses and location details.
  • Your order history and order details stored in your account.
  • Your wishlist, cart and app preferences on the device.
  • Data stored only on your device is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.
